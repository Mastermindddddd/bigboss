# Asset and reference notes

- The original BigBoss identity and the V2 executive and operations dashboard artwork come from the two ZIPs provided by the user. V2 artwork is presented as numbered template previews; embedded names and metrics are design content, not BigBoss client claims.
- The three WhatsApp videos supplied on 16 November 2026 were reviewed at multiple timestamps. Their glass surfaces, dense application layouts, contextual panels and animated navigation informed the redesign. The videos, creator handles, source code shown in them and audio are not redistributed in this package.
- The geographic land geometry uses Natural Earth 1:110m land data, simplified into a local SVG path for this website. [Natural Earth terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) place the map data in the public domain. [Source geometry](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson).
- Icons: the existing project's `lucide-react` dependency.
- New workspace layouts, glass styling, procedural hero scenes and interactions are implemented in the website source. Dashboard data is fictional preview data. No client backend, GPU service or vendor partnership is implied.
